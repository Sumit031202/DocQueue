# DocQueue

**DocQueue** is a SaaS platform designed to improve transparency between doctors and patients by providing **real-time visibility into patient queues**.

Patients can see their current position in the queue, get an estimate of their waiting time, and track queue updates in real time. Doctors can manage their queues and provide patients with clearer information about expected waiting times.

---

## ✨ Features

### For Patients

* 📍 View their current position in the queue
* ⏱️ Estimate how long they may need to wait
* 🔄 Track queue updates in real time

### For Doctors

* 🩺 Manage patient queues
* 📋 Monitor the current queue
* ⏳ Provide patients with clearer waiting-time information

---

## 🎯 Motivation

A common problem in clinics is the uncertainty around waiting times.

Patients may arrive at a clinic without knowing:

* How many people are ahead of them
* How long they are likely to wait
* Whether the queue is moving or delayed

**DocQueue** aims to make this process more transparent by giving patients real-time visibility into the queue.

The goal is to reduce unnecessary waiting, improve communication, and create a smoother experience for both doctors and patients.
